# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

KubeFit is committed to a respectful, harassment-free, and technically constructive
community. Be welcoming to contributors of different backgrounds and experience
levels, critique ideas rather than people, accept responsibility for mistakes, and
keep project discussions focused on improving the software and its documentation.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, code reviews,
project discussions, and public spaces where someone is officially representing
KubeFit.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately
through a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sangmu1126). Include the relevant
links and a concise description. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, and the maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently exclude a participant according to severity and
context.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard and must
avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing a report.

## Attribution

This project adopts the principles and enforcement approach of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The project-specific text above keeps the reporting route and current maintainer
responsibility explicit.
